What it covers

A quieter way to run spend, expenses, and approvals.

Built for operators who inherited three tools, a shared inbox, and a spreadsheet named FINAL_v7.

Cards and invoices in one ledger

Corporate cards, reimbursements, and vendor bills land in the same view so nobody is reconciling across tabs at 6pm.

Policy that people can actually follow

Thresholds, categories, and entity rules sit next to the request — not in a PDF last updated two fiscal years ago.

Approvals that move

Route by team, amount, or vendor. Nudges exist. They are polite. They are also persistent.

A close that is less of a scavenger hunt

Coding, receipts, and exceptions stay attached to the spend so controllers are not chasing Slack threads.
